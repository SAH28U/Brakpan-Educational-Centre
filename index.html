About Us

Established • Independent

Brakpan Educational Centre focuses on developing learners spiritually, intellectually, physically, culturally and socially. We emphasise individual attention, small classes and a caring environment supported by experienced staff. Our school has maintained a tradition of academic excellence while fostering values of faith, hope, and charity. Our bilingual approach ensures students are prepared for both local and global opportunities. Brakpan Educational Centre is an independent combined school of excellence with a dynamic, happy learning environment and outstanding learning facilities to enable our learners to reach their full potential academically, physically, socially and spiritually. Qualified experienced teachers with passion and enthusiasm fulfill the needs of each child in a sensitive caring manner supported by a Christian value system. Graduates of Brakpan Educational Centre will thus emerge as fully rounded individuals molded by the School's ethos and opportunities, ready to face life's challenges.

It is the vision of Brakpan Educational Centre to help develop each learner to his or her full potential spiritually, intellectually, physically, culturally and socially. We focus on the learners cognitive development by challenging them to think, to reason, deliberate and form personal opinions based on the available information given to them. They are taught the necessary skills to manage the information with the best technology possible. We believe in helping the learners develop a sense of personal style and self worth by providing a strong school ethos, a strong value system and sense of history and tradition. We also provide every learner with the life skills to enable him or her to focus on clear attainable personal goals.
